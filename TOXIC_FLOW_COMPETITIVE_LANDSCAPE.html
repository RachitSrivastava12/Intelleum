Solana Toxic Order-Flow Buyer Map

Research snapshot prepared May 8, 2026. The market is crowded with swap routers, wallets, DEXs, lending protocols, launchpads, and trading terminals, but sparse for a neutral pre-trade toxic-flow firewall that turns detection into policy, enforcement, and provable savings.

Core thesis

Intelleum should not pitch itself as another MEV dashboard. The winning angle is: stop toxic execution before value leaks, then prove the dollars saved. Existing products protect parts of the stack: Jupiter manages swap execution, Jito provides protected submission primitives, wallets warn on token/security risks, and venues expose analytics. Intelleum can sit across them as the risk and policy layer.

Priority Buyer Segments

01 / Routers + wallets

Jupiter, Phantom, Solflare, Backpack

Need route-level warnings, protected execution defaults, and clear user-facing savings.

02 / AMMs + LP venues

Raydium, Meteora, Orca, PumpSwap

Need LP adverse-selection scoring, JIT add/remove detection, and fee/size guardrails.

03 / Perps + lending

Drift, Kamino, Save, marginfi

Need liquidation regime detection, maker-quality scoring, and oracle/liquidity stress monitors.

04 / Trading terminals

Axiom, Photon, Trojan, BONKbot, BullX

Need high-slippage memecoin protection, route vetoes, and copy-trade MEV exposure scoring.

05 / MEV + infra

Jito, Helius, QuickNode, Chainstack

Need risk scores and execution-policy APIs they can bundle into RPC, streams, and protected send paths.

06 / Public benchmark

Solana ecosystem

Needs a shared Toxic Flow Index for venues, validators, pools, and entity clusters.
